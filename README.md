## 📊 Visualizations Implemented  

### 1. Treemap (Company Budget Distribution)  
- Used to represent hierarchical data.  
- Shows how company budget is divided among departments.  
- Larger area = higher budget allocation.  

👉 Insight: IT department has the highest budget allocation.

---

### 2. Dendrogram (Hierarchical Clustering)  
- Used to visualize clustering relationships between data points.  
- Based on distance between points.  

👉 Insight: Closely linked branches indicate similar data points.

---

### 3. Venn Diagram  
- Used to show relationship between two sets.  
- Displays intersection and unique elements.  

👉 Insight: Elements 3 and 4 are common in both sets.

---

### 4. Sankey Diagram (Student Flow)  
- Represents flow of data from one stage to another.  
- Width of flow indicates quantity.  

👉 Insight: Shows reduction in student count from admission to placement.

---

### 5. 3D Scatter Plot (Student Performance)  
- Visualizes relationship among three variables:
  - Study Hours  
  - Marks  
  - Attendance  

👉 Insight: Higher study hours and attendance generally lead to better marks.

---

### 6. Radar Chart (Skill Assessment)  
- Displays multivariate data in circular form.  
- Used for performance comparison across skills.  

👉 Insight: Strong performance in DBMS and DSA, moderate in ML and Communication.
